# Fresh Animations integration

This local test build combines Fresh Animations v1.10.5 and the user-supplied Quivers v2.2, Creepers v2.1, Spiders v2.2, Emissive v1.6, Objects v2.1.2, Details v2.3, and All Extensions v1.9.2 archives. It also includes the supplied Fresh Animations x Baby Animals Remastered v0.1, FA+VillagerNews v1.0, and FA+Player v1.1 archives. The original Villager News villager models are preserved. The Fresh Animations player rig provides movement, idle, equipment, first-person, and armor models. Its outer skin layers are animated with their matching body parts, and the vanilla cape renderer remains in control. The pillager model is adapted so its existing mouth and head and body animation channels respond to the mod's dialogue timing. The Villager News models retain this mod's dialogue gaze expressions.

The import script preserves the original Villager News CEM models, imports the supplied baby models and textures for chickens, mooshrooms, sheep, and wolves, and places both Fresh Animations classic and slim player models where EMF can load them. It selects the archive's 1.21.5+ player variable file for Minecraft 26.3 and removes a player face reference whose target model is absent from the supplied archive. The temperate chicken baby model is used as the warm-chicken variant fallback. A separate crouch sway change is not part of this player animation build. The supplied baby archive contains no piglet or calf models. These assets are bundled for private testing with Minecraft 26.3 and EMF.

The supplied archive terms allow personal modifications and prohibit public sharing of modified packs, redistribution of the pack, and inclusion of unedited assets in another resource pack without permission. Obtain permission from FreshLX before publicly distributing a build containing these assets. The official project page states that its online terms supersede the copies inside the archives (FreshLX, n.d.).

# Changelog

## 1.4.12

- Add 69 new pillager dialogue recordings and four pillager spawn-egg lines,
  for 121 pillager lines total.
- Add pillager reactions for waving, patrol interactions, player equipment,
  villager arrow hits, and other combat situations.
- Make nearby pillagers raise and wave their left arms for wave-related lines;
  the speaking pillager stays still.
- Convert pillager and Vex recordings to mono for positional playback, and add
  five Vex voice lines.
- Improve Fresh Animations compatibility for the player, skin layers, and
  Essentials cosmetics, including the crouch waddle.
- Restore the Baby Animals Remastered model compatibility, including the baby
  sheep leg and wool alignment.
- Refine villager faces, dialogue gestures, and animation compatibility.
